# User guide

## Preserved overview

[PharmaPath](https://pharmapath.org) preserves an overview of the former project. The project concluded on October 11, 2026. Product actions display a retirement notice; they do not search pharmacies, look up medications, sign in, save information, or submit forms.

The overview is not a source of current medication or shortage information. Previous descriptions of search, watchlists, accounts, and notifications describe services that are no longer available.

## Prepare a pharmacy call

Contact pharmacies directly. Have the medication name, strength, and dosage form ready, and ask about:

- Whether the pharmacy can fill the exact prescription.
- The final price and insurance coverage.
- Pickup timing and any transfer requirements.

Confirm these details before traveling. Discuss treatment decisions and substitutions with a qualified clinician or pharmacist.
